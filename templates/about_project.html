Health Informatics Project

Diabetes Risk Prediction System (DiaGuard.ai)

An accessible web-based preventive health screener designed to evaluate an individual's early risk indicators for Type 2 diabetes using non-invasive lifestyle parameters and machine learning models trained on clinical biomarker data.

Medical Disclaimer: This is not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a doctor. This project is built for educational demonstration and screening prototype purposes.
Problem Statement

Type 2 diabetes often develops silently over years without noticeable discomfort. Millions of individuals live with elevated blood sugar (prediabetes) without knowing it, missing the crucial window where lifestyle adjustments can reverse or halt disease progression.

Traditional clinical laboratory testing requires scheduled visits, needle draws, and expense. Providing a simple, accessible early screener allows individuals to identify their personal risk factors early and seek timely medical evaluation.

Technology Stack

Built using a lightweight, performant stack focused on interpretable machine learning and responsive interfaces:

Python Flask scikit-learn SQLite Bootstrap Chart.js
Dataset Used

Pima Indians Diabetes Database: A widely recognized clinical benchmark dataset consisting of 768 patient records with 8 biological markers (Pregnancies, Glucose, Blood Pressure, Skin Thickness, Insulin, BMI, Diabetes Pedigree, and Age) used to train, test, and benchmark candidate classification algorithms.

How the Prediction Works in 4 Simple Steps
1
Questionnaire

The user enters everyday lifestyle factors, age, family history, and symptoms without medical jargon.

2
BMI Calculation

Height and weight inputs instantly compute Body Mass Index (BMI) and assign the clinical category.

3
Model Prediction

The trained machine learning classifier evaluates biomarker weights to compute an estimated risk score.

4
Risk Level with Tips

Outputs a categorized risk score (Low, Medium, High) with top contributing factors and diet/exercise tips.

Key Features of the Project
  • Dual Assessment Modes: Plain-language lifestyle questionnaire (Quick Check) and lab marker ML classifier (Detailed Check).
  • Explainable Predictions: Identifies and highlights the top 3 contributing factors with one-line biological explanations.
  • Dynamic Chart.js Visualizations: Semi-circle risk score gauge, horizontal BMI comparison chart, and history risk trend lines.
  • Personalized 7-Day Plans: Tailored diet and exercise suggestions with an instant English / Tamil language toggle.
  • Downloadable Clinical PDF: One-click generation of a clean clinical summary report for physician review.
  • Local History & Admin Dashboard: SQLite-backed assessment history logging and administrator metric analytics.
