Frequently Asked Questions

Clear and honest answers about how this system works, how accurate it is, and how your personal health data is handled.

Medical Disclaimer: This is not a medical diagnosis. Please consult a doctor. This application is an educational early screening aid.

Our machine learning model is trained on clinical data and achieves approximately 75%–80% testing accuracy. However, predictions are statistical estimates of risk based on general patterns—they are screening indicators, not laboratory diagnoses. A low score does not guarantee immunity, and a high score does not mean you definitely have diabetes.

Assessment results are stored strictly in a local SQLite database on this server for the History table and your optional PDF report download. We do not share, sell, or transmit your personal details to third parties or cloud servers. You can clear your assessment history at any time from the History page.

Clinical diabetes can only be confirmed through certified laboratory diagnostic tests ordered by a physician, such as Fasting Plasma Glucose (FPG), Oral Glucose Tolerance Test (OGTT), or an HbA1c blood test.

Body Mass Index (BMI) is an indicator calculated from your height and weight (weight in kg / height in meters²). Excess body weight—especially when BMI is over 25 (Overweight) or 30 (Obese)—is one of the leading modifiable risk factors for Type 2 diabetes because excess fat tissue interferes with how effectively your cells respond to insulin.

Do not panic! A high score simply indicates that you have multiple risk factors (such as BMI, age, symptoms, or genetic history). Schedule a checkup with a healthcare provider or diabetologist to get an official fasting blood glucose or HbA1c test. You can also download your summary PDF report to discuss it with your doctor.

Our Detailed Lab Check classifier is trained on the benchmark Pima Indians Diabetes Database (National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases). It includes 768 patient records with 8 clinical features (Glucose, Blood Pressure, Skin Thickness, Insulin, BMI, Diabetes Pedigree, and Age), preprocessed to impute missing values and normalized with standard scaling.
