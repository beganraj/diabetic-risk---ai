Viva & Theoretical Insights
1. Logistic Regression A linear probabilistic model. It serves as an interpretable baseline but cannot capture complex non-linear feature interactions between glucose, insulin, and BMI without extensive feature engineering.
2. Decision Tree A non-linear tree-based classifier that creates hierarchical threshold rules (e.g. Glucose > 127). Highly interpretable and effective for rule extraction, achieving top accuracy on test partitions.
3. Random Forest An ensemble of bootstrapped decision trees with feature sub-sampling. Reduces variance and prevents overfitting, making it a reliable standard for clinical risk profiling.
